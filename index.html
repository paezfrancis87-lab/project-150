Because High School Is Tough Enough...

Without having to worry about where you are going to sleep at night, where your next meal is coming from, and how you will pay for clothing, hygiene items, and school supplies.

Project 150 was created to help homeless, displaced, and disadvantaged high school students stay in school and thrive.

Our Mission

To provide free support and services to homeless, displaced, and disadvantaged high school students so they can remain in school, graduate, and build bright futures.

Our Programs

Food Assistance

Providing meals and groceries for students facing food insecurity.

School Supplies

Helping students stay prepared with backpacks and educational materials.

Clothing Support

Giving students access to clothing and hygiene essentials.
